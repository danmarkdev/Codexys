About Codexys

We're a small team dedicated to helping businesses launch and run on the web — through website development, UI/UX design, and the systems that keep their operations running.

Our Mission

To give businesses a web presence that actually does its job — fast, clear, and built to grow with them.

Small, Focused Team

A founder, two developers, and a designer — each hands-on with every project we take.

Built With Current Tools

Modern frameworks and practices — nothing bolted on just because it's trendy.

Quality Assured

Every site, interface, and system is reviewed and tested before it reaches your users.

What We Offer

Our Services

We offer a focused set of web services to help your business launch, look sharp, and run smoothly.

Website Development

Marketing sites, product sites, and storefronts built to load fast, hold up under traffic, and stay easy to update.

UI/UX Design

Interfaces designed around how people actually move through a task, not how a template happens to be laid out.

Web Systems & Dashboards

Internal tools and dashboards that replace spreadsheets and manual steps with something your team actually opens.
